Yes — with the right finish and protection. See our Sofitel Foshan bathroom project: floor, walls and vanity top in Lake Baikal (Monet Green), a ≤0.2% absorption finish. In practice: six-side sealing, proper drainage slope, watertight joints, and anti-mold sealant at counter-to-wall seams. Travertines (Milk White Travertine, Roman Travertine) have open pores — fill and seal carefully before wet-area use.

Three essentials: 1) full six-side sealing; 2) low-alkali cement and dedicated adhesive, never lay on wet substrate; 3) drainage and waterproofing with ventilation at wall bases. Basements and exterior-adjacent walls need back-side damp-proofing. We can pre-treat slabs at the factory and advise on the laying process.

For in-stock slabs, standard processes (cutting, edge profiling, bevel) take about 3–12 working days depending on order size and process combination. Work combining drilling, shaping, six-side sealing or anti-slip surfaces is confirmed in writing with a schedule before ordering, with progress updates at each milestone.

Tell us the area and location first — we calculate real slab yield, which usually saves stone compared with estimating from floor area.

Every slab is numbered and photographed before dispatch, so a "wrong veining" claim can be checked slab by slab — responsibility is clear. Damage & replacement terms in full:
1. Report window: within 7 days of arrival, with the unboxing video, crate number and slab numbers.
2. In-transit damage: breakage within the written 3–5% allowance is normal transit loss; damage beyond the allowance is replaced free of charge from same-batch remainder slabs (replacement freight is buyer's cost) or credited pro-rata against the balance — your choice.
3. Wrong veining: settled by slab number against the pre-crating photo set — if a delivered slab does not match its approved numbered photo, we replace it or credit it.
4. Reorder matching: we reserve same-batch remainder slabs for you before any other customer — order full quantity and keep ~5% spare to guarantee tone continuity.
