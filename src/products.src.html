PRODUCTS · SELF-OPERATED RANGE

15 finishes.

Every finish below is self-operated — slabs in stock at our Yunfu yard, selectable by number. Gray-led range with white and beige complements; data shown is the reference for the actual slabs you pick.

01

Slab Sizes

Standard slabs start around 2400×1600mm; cut-to-size available — floor tiles, countertops, stair treads to drawing.

02

Thickness

Stock thickness 18–20mm across the range; some finishes also available thinner. Edge profiling and beveling in-house.

03

Absorption

Water absorption 0.2%–0.5% by finish — wet-area options like Lake Baikal at ≤0.2% with six-side sealing.
